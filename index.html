The problem

The peptide industry has a trust problem.

Grey-market peptides look cheap on paper. In practice, that price gets you weeks of international shipping, zero documentation of what's actually in the vial, and no recourse if it turns out underdosed or mislabelled. The saving isn't worth the risk.

On the other side, plenty of local suppliers lean on the word "Australian" to justify a price that has nothing to do with what's in the vial — steep margins, thin documentation, and quality claims with no paperwork behind them.

Aciona sits in neither camp. We work with established manufacturing partners, verify every batch through an independent laboratory, and price our products at what they're actually worth — not at what the market will bear.
